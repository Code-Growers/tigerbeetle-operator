The TigerBeetle Operator has been installed.

1. Verify the operator is running:

   kubectl get deployment {{ include "tigerbeetle-operator.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Create a TigerBeetle cluster:

   kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
   apiVersion: tigerbeetle.codegrowers.com/v1alpha1
   kind: TigerBeetleCluster
   metadata:
     name: my-tigerbeetle
   spec:
     # A random 128-bit decimal integer; generate one with:
     #   echo "ibase=16; $(openssl rand -hex 16 | tr a-f A-F)" | bc
     clusterID: "<random u128>"
     replicas: 1
     storage:
       size: 10Gi
     resources:
       limits:
         memory: 8Gi
   EOF

3. Check the status of your cluster:

   kubectl get tigerbeetleclusters -A
   kubectl describe tigerbeetlecluster my-tigerbeetle
